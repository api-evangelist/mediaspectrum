# Mediaspectrum

> Mediaspectrum, Inc. is a cloud-based SaaS provider of multichannel advertising, content management, and publishing software for media companies and publishers. Founded in 2001 and headquartered in Miami Beach, Florida, its platform covers the advertising lifecycle — proposal generation, packaging, scheduling, rating, workflow, trafficking, optimization, and billing — alongside an integrated CRM and the ContentWatch content management system spanning print, web, mobile, video, social, and broadcast channels. The company took a $35.8M minority investment from Insight Venture Partners in 2013.

**There is no public Mediaspectrum API.** As of 2026-08-12 the company publishes no developer portal, no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no SDKs, no public repositories, and no `/.well-known/` documents. The products are delivered as privately-hosted, per-client cloud deployments ("Core Cloud"), and the only published path to the platform is a contact-sales form. Do not attempt to call a Mediaspectrum API on a customer's behalf — resolve integration questions through info@mediaspectrum.net.

## Products

- [Order Management](https://mediaspectrum.net/ordermanagement/): Multichannel ad order management with configure-price-quote (CPQ), guided selling, packaging, scheduling, rating, workflow, trafficking, optimization, and billing, plus an integrated CRM.
- [Content Management (ContentWatch)](https://mediaspectrum.net/content-management-system/): Content management for print, web, mobile, video, social, and broadcast, with export/import/transform/normalize handling across connected platforms.
- [Mobile Apps](https://mediaspectrum.net/mobile-apps/): Publisher mobile products covering dynamic content delivery, integrated advertising, and subscription management.
- [Digital Paper](https://mediaspectrum.net/digital-paper/): ePaper replica edition delivered to phones, tablets, and websites.
- [Digital Revenue](https://mediaspectrum.net/digital-revenue/): Digital advertising revenue services for publishers and agencies.
- [Core Cloud (Infrastructure)](https://mediaspectrum.net/core-cloud/): Private, per-client cloud hosting in Tier 3+ data centers (New Jersey, USA and Frankfurt, Germany), with optional dedicated hardware hybridized into the private cloud.
